Executive Summary HospitalOS currently manages inventory only for the pharmacy: medicines have a master, batch-level stock with expiry, purchase orders, GRNs, purchase invoices with 3-way matching, returns, and ward stock for medicines. There is no system of record for everything else a hospital consumes daily â€” IV fluids, glucose, bandages, syringes, gloves, sutures, catheters, linen, stationery, housekeeping and maintenance materials, lab consumables beyond reagents, and OT consumables. This PRD specifies a production-grade, hospital-wide Inventory & Materials Management module: a generic item master, a central store with department sub-stores, the full procure-to-pay cycle (requisition â†’ PO â†’ GRN â†’ purchase invoice), indent-based internal issues, stock transfers, batch/expiry control with FEFO, physical and cycle counting, reorder automation, moving weighted average valuation with a perpetual stock ledger, and ABC/VED analytics â€” fully integrated with the existing Finance/GL module so every stock movement that has a financial effect posts a journal entry. The module must be built inside the existing HospitalOS architecture and conventions (Next.js App Router, Prisma/PostgreSQL, server actions, organization-scoped multi-tenancy) and must reuse â€” not duplicate â€” the pharmacy procurement pipeline, the unified Vendor master, and the GL posting framework in app/actions/gl-actions.ts. 2. The existing PurchaseOrder, PurchaseOrderItem, GoodsReceiptNote and PharmacyPurchaseInvoice tables are extended with a nullable item_id alongside the existing medicine_id (exactly one must be set per line), plus a store_id on PO and GRN headers for the receiving store. Vendor returns: generalize PharmacyReturn with item support â€” supplier_return, expired_stock and damage_writeoff types; debit-note generation against vendor payable. 5.4 Indents & Internal Issues (FR-INV-04) Indent: requesting store/department + cost center raises an indent on a source store (default: parent store); lines with item, qty, priority (NORMAL/URGENT/EMERGENCY), optional patient/admission reference for patient-specific requests; statuses: Draft â†’ Submitted â†’ Approved â†’ Partially Issued â†’ Issued â†’ Closed/Rejected/Cancelled. Issue against indent: the issuing store picks stock with FEFO-enforced batch suggestions (reusing the DispenseAllocation allocation pattern); short-supply allowed with reason; movement OUT at issuing store and IN-TRANSIT or direct IN at receiving store (two-step acknowledgment for inter-branch, one-step for intra-branch, configurable). Direct consumption issue: for non-stocking departments, an issue can be expensed directly to the department cost center without creating sub-store stock. Transfer of batch-tracked items always carries batch identity and expiry. 5.7 Physical Count, Cycle Count & Adjustments (FR-INV-07) Count session: scope = store + optional category/ABC filter; system snapshots book quantities at freeze time; count sheets (printable + mobile entry); blind count option (counters do not see book qty); recount workflow for variances above tolerance. A cycle count with a shortage above tolerance requires finance approval and, once approved, posts an ADJUSTMENT movement and shrinkage journal; rejected variances change nothing. Expiry of a quarantined batch can be written off only by finance approval and posts the write-off journal; the expiry register no longer shows it as available.
